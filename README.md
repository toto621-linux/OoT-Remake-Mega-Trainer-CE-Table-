# OoT-Remake-Mega-Trainer-CE-Table-
This Cheat Engine table lets you modify Link's resources, inventory, and equipment while playing **The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Switch 2)**.

## Tested Configuration

All options have been tested in-game and confirmed working with **Emulator**. Majora's Mask was tested with version **1.0.2** of its mod.

## How to Use

1. Launch the game and load your save.
2. Open [OoT_Remake_Mega_Trainer.ct](OoT_Remake_Mega_Trainer.ct) in Cheat Engine and attach it to the right process.
3. Check **ENABLE** or press **Numpad 0**. Wait for detection to finish before enabling any other options. The table scans for addresses specific to the current session.

## Infinite Options

| Hotkey | Option | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Numpad 1 | **Infinite Hearts** | Refills hearts up to the current maximum. |
| Numpad 2 | **Infinite Magic** | Refills the active magic meter up to the highest unlocked capacity. |
| Numpad 3 | **Infinite Rupees** | Refills the wallet up to its current capacity. |
| Numpad 4 | **Infinite Consumables** | Replenishes Deku Seeds, arrows, bombs, Bombchus, Deku Nuts, Pest Nuts, Magic Beans, and Deku Sticks. Each consumable must be unlocked, with at least one remaining. |
| Numpad 5 | **Infinite Bottle Use** | Restores the contents after Link finishes putting the bottle away. A bottle that was initially empty remains empty. |

## Status and Inventory

- **Status** lets you edit hearts, rupees, wallet capacity, consumable quantities, available abilities, Heart Pieces, and Gold Skulltula tokens. Magic is displayed in tenths of a point. Double-click **Magic Meter** to unlock or upgrade the meter to **24 or 48 MP**. Bombs and Bombchus share the same capacity.
- **Inventory** includes tools, quest items, rewards, and upgrades. Double-click an item's value to obtain or remove it. Small Keys can be edited per dungeon for the entries included in the table.
- **Ocarina's songs** contains all **12 songs**. Each entry has its own **Locked / Unlocked** dropdown menu.
- **Bottles** lets you manage all five bottles individually and choose the contents of each owned bottle.
- **Equipment and Masks** includes weapons, shields, outfits, and masks. **Inventory Mask Slots** provides two independent dropdown menus: one for regular masks and one for amiibo masks. Double-click the value of the slot you want to change.

## Majora's Mask

Install and enable the **Majora's Mask – New Wearable Mask & Side Quest** mod before loading your save. Once the table detects the mod, you can unlock the mask under **Equipment and Masks → Amiibo Masks** or select it under **Inventory Mask Slots → Amiibo Mask Slot**. [Download the mod from GameBanana](https://gamebanana.com/mods/740716).
